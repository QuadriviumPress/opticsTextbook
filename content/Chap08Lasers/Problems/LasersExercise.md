# Problems

**Problem 8.1** Short Questions on Lasers.

**(a)** A ring laser is composed of 4 identical mirrors with a reflection coefficient of 0.99. Give the value of the gain that is necessary in the amplifier medium to obtain sustained lasing.

**(b)** Give the frequency difference between two longitudinal modes in a linear cavity whose optical length is L = 300mm.

**(c)** Imagine that one of the mirrors in a two mirror cavity is not perfect and has a complex index of refraction (such as gold for example with n=0.25+3i for 600 nm). How does it affect the mode in the laser?

**Problem 8.2** The helium-neon gain medium has a spectral band of amplification of 1 GHz at 633 nm. It is assumed that the spectral profile is rectangular to simplify. The linear cavity of the laser has a length of 30 cm. Give the number of longitudinal modes that can oscillate in this cavity.

**Problem 8.3** A pulsed laser produces pulses of 10 $\mu$J with a duration of 1 ns. The pulse repetition frequency is 10 kHz. Give the peak power of the pulses and the average output power of the laser.
